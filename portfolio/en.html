Personal Assistant

A deadline that lives only in your head is a deadline you will miss.

A personal deadline assistant. You send a note by text or by voice — “appointment with Ana, Friday at 2pm” — and the system reads the date, schedules the reminder ladder you chose (2 days, 1 day, 1 hour, 15 minutes) and pushes to your phone’s lock screen with the app closed.

Decisions that mattered

  • AI is off the critical path. Dates are read by a hand-written, deterministic Portuguese parser that costs nothing and covers “Friday at 2pm”, “the 20th”, “tomorrow morning” and “in two weeks”. The Claude API only steps in when a note carries no recognisable date, and one environment variable turns AI off entirely. With no API key at all, the app still works end to end.
  • Pulling a reminder off the queue is atomic in the database. DELETE … RETURNING with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED — that is what stops two ticks from notifying the same deadline. Read-then-delete would have been simpler, and would have double-notified.
  • The API runs in a Supabase Edge Function, not on Vercel. Inside it the service key already exists in the environment, so the project deploys without anyone registering a single variable. Scheduling is Postgres’s own pg_cron — no external scheduler, no paid tier.
  • There is no public sign-up, and that is deliberate. This is a personal assistant that pushes notifications to my own phone’s lock screen, not a SaaS: a new account is created by SQL. The sign-in screen still rate-limits to five attempts per origin, and being locked out beats the correct password — checking the credential first would make the limit decorative.

R2D Execution Report

The plan is already approved. What is missing is proof of execution.

A field tool for pharmaceutical sales forces. It reads the approved plan PDF purely as reference, records the actions actually taken (date, action, point of sale or doctor, outcome, photos) and the month-by-month indicators, then closes a three-page PDF report for management — numbers first, then objective and gap, then the actions in chronological order.

Decisions that mattered

  • The extractor deliberately discards the indicator section of the PDF. Reading market share out of the plan would mean inventing a number that travels up to management. The tool stores, compares and charts — it does not calculate, estimate or fill in any indicator. The difference between two values you typed is a comparison; anything beyond that would be a guess wearing the costume of data.
  • No CDN. pdf.js, html2canvas, jsPDF and the typeface are vendored and cached by the service worker. The real setting is a pharmacy aisle and a clinic waiting room: the app has to open with no signal, or it is good for nothing.
  • No sign-up, no login, no server. Everything lives in the browser’s IndexedDB. The trade-off was taken head-on rather than hidden: JSON backup and restore with the photos embedded, right at the top of the screen.

Finances Control

It is not a discipline problem. It is a visibility problem.

A personal finance app with a visual dashboard and per-category diagnosis: it shows in real time where the money is going, flags critical spending, tracks a savings goal and lays out the month’s budget.

Decisions that mattered

  • Diagnosis, not a bank statement. Any spreadsheet can list expenses. The value is in naming the category that blew up and how far the goal still is — the reading, not the data.

WhatsApp Assistant

80% of your inbound is predictable. None of it needed you.

A conversational assistant on WhatsApp that answers, resolves questions, collects information and books appointments around the clock, with no human in the loop. Only the already-qualified contact reaches a person.

Decisions that mattered

02 — Method

Four rules I do not break.

These are not style preferences. Each one was learned expensively, on a project listed on this page.

01

Zero cost is a requirement, not a detail.

What I build runs on free tiers end to end. Not out of elegance: a project that needs an invoice to keep existing dies the first month nobody looks at it.

02

AI stays off the critical path.

The core is deterministic and testable. AI comes in as reinforcement, stays optional, and can be switched off with an environment variable. If the key disappears, the product stays whole.

03

Offline is the setting, not the exception.

A pharmacy aisle, a waiting room, the side of a court. If an app only works on a good connection, it does not work where it is going to be used.

04

I deliver the problem solved, not the request fulfilled.

The request is the symptom someone managed to name. My job is to understand the operation deeply enough to see what sits behind it — and, sometimes, to say that what was asked for will not fix it.
